TAXONOMY REFERENCE: NATURE AXIS

INCLUSION SCOPE:
- More-Than-Human Living Entities (Flora, Fauna, Fungi): Wild, domesticated, cultivated, or bred non-human organisms (e.g., plants, trees, flowers, insects, birds, mammals, reptiles, cultivated mushrooms, bacteria).
- Ecosystems & Environments: Natural or semi-natural environments where non-human entities exist and interact (e.g., forests, coral reefs, nature reserves, urban parks, vegetable gardens).
- Non-Living Natural Elements & Processes:
  * Hydrological: Rivers, ponds, lakes, oceans, streams.
  * Atmospheric & Meteorological: Rain, wind, snow, fog, temperature, natural seasons (e.g., "hot summer").
  * Geological & Mineral: Rocks, soil, sand, salt, mineral oil, petrol.
  * Astrophysical: Sunsets, sunrises, stars, moon, eclipses, natural day/night cycles.
  * Biophysical: Photosynthesis, erosion, evapotranspiration.
- Processed Food & Drinks (The Ingestion Criterion): Edible solid or liquid elements at any processing level (e.g., pasta, cooked meats, canned tuna, sugar, coffee, butter, liqueur).
- Nature-Based Artefacts: Crafted or manufactured objects where the natural material of origin remains visually identifiable by its structure, grain, or texture (e.g., wooden tables with visible wood grain, stone surfaces with distinct natural texture).
- Nature-Based Activities: Physical activities explicitly taking place within or directly engaging with natural environments or elements (e.g., hiking, surfing, nature-based sports, gardening, fishing).
- Representations of Nature: Real, stylized, abstract, artistic, or fictional depictions that explicitly reference or depict any of the more-than-human categories above (e.g., landscape paintings, cartoon animals, video-game forests, poetic metaphors, figurines or statues of fauna and flora).

EXCLUSION SCOPE:
- Human Beings: Human individuals, human body parts, or groups of people are NOT classified as nature under this taxonomy.
- Homogenous Artefacts: Manufactured structural or functional objects where the original natural texture is unidentifiable and the object does not depict a natural entity (e.g., a plain column, a chair painted solid blue with no visible wood grain). Explicit animal or plant depictions (such as figurines or statues of fauna and flora) are NOT excluded here; they count as Representations of Nature.
- Food & Drink Packaging: Containers, wrappers, cans, or boxes, unless the text name or an image of the natural element inside is explicitly printed on the package exterior.
- Administrative & Political Units: Geopolitical entities, boundaries, or labels (e.g., countries, counties, districts, cities), even if they are named after natural features.
- Accidental or Contingent Meanings: Uses of natural terms that do not refer to natural characteristics (e.g., "summer school" or "last summer" as a calendar reference, or "good morning/night" as a conversational greeting).
