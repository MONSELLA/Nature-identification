TAXONOMY REFERENCE: NATURE AXIS

INCLUSION SCOPE:
- More-Than-Human Living Entities (Flora, Fauna, Fungi): Wild, domesticated, cultivated, or bred non-human organisms (for example, plants, animals, trees, flowers, insects, birds, mammals, reptiles, mushrooms, and bacteria).
- Ecosystems and Environments: Natural or semi-natural environments where the more-than-human living entities exist and interact (for example, forests, coral reefs, nature reserves, urban parks, vegetable gardens, and beaches).
- Non-Living Natural Elements:
  * Hydrological: For example, rivers, ponds, lakes, oceans, streams.
  * Atmospheric and Meteorological: For example, rain, clouds, snow, wind, and sky.
  * Geological and Topographical: For example, mountains, canyons, cliffs, valleys, caves, dunes, rocks, sand, salt, mineral oil.
  * Astrophysical: For example, sun, stars, moon, planets.
- Processed Food and Drinks: Edible solid or liquid elements at any processing level (for example, pasta, seafood, cooked meats, canned tuna, butter, milk, and salad).
- Nature-Based Artefacts: Crafted or manufactured objects where the natural material of origin remains visually identifiable by its structure, grain, or texture (for example, wooden tables or tools with visible wood grain and houses made of stone or wood where the natural texture of these materials is clearly identifiable).
- Representations of Nature: Real, stylized, abstract, artistic, scientific or fictional depictions that explicitly reference or depict any of the more-than-human categories above (for example, emojis of flora and fauna, landscape paintings, cartoon or stuffed animals and plants, video-game forests, and figurines or statues of fauna and flora).

EXCLUSION SCOPE:
- Human Beings: Human individuals, human body parts, or groups of people are NOT classified as nature under this taxonomy.
- Homogenous Artefacts: Manufactured objects that do not have an identifiable natural texture and that do not depict a natural entity of the ones described above are completely excluded (for example, a plastic fan, a metal lamp, a plain column with no natural texture visible, a desk painted solid blue with no visible wood grain). Explicit animal or plant depictions (such as figurines, statues or stuffed toys of fauna and flora) are NOT excluded here; they count as Representations of Nature.
- Food and Drink Packaging: Containers, wrappers, cans, or boxes, unless the text name or an image of the natural element inside is explicitly printed on the package exterior.
