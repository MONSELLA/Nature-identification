TAXONOMY REFERENCE: NATURE AXIS

INCLUSION SCOPE:
- More-Than-Human Living Entities (Flora, Fauna, Fungi): Wild, domesticated, cultivated, or bred non-human organisms (for example, plants, animals, trees, flowers, insects, birds, mammals, reptiles, mushrooms, and bacteria).
- Ecosystems and Environments: Natural or semi-natural environments where the more-than-human living entities exist and interact (for example, forests, coral reefs, nature reserves, urban parks, vegetable gardens, and beaches).
- Non-Living Natural Elements:
  * Hydrological: For example, rivers, ponds, lakes, oceans, streams.
  * Atmospheric and Meteorological: For example, rain, clouds, snow, wind, and sky.
  * Geological and Topographical: For example, mountains, canyons, cliffs, valleys, caves, dunes, rocks, sand, salt, mineral oil.
  * Astrophysical: For example, sun, stars, moon, planets.
- Processed Food and Drinks: Edible solid or liquid elements at any processing level (for example, pasta, seafood, cooked meats, canned tuna, butter, milk, and salad).
- Nature-Based Artefacts: Crafted or manufactured objects where the natural material of origin remains visually identifiable by its structure, grain, or texture (for example, wooden tables or tools, houses made of stone or wood).
- Representations of Nature: Real, stylized, abstract, artistic, scientific, fictional or graphic depictions that explicitly reference or depict any of the more-than-human categories above (for example, emojis of fauna and flora, landscape paintings, cartoon or stuffed animals and plants, video-game forests, and figurines or statues of fauna and flora).
  * NOTE: The nature representation MUST resemble the nature entity being represented. For example, a dragon can be correctly identified as a large lizard. However, some representations do not aim to resemble nature, such as the furry puppet Elmo.

EXCLUSION SCOPE:
- Human Beings: Human individuals, human body parts (for example, skull, heart, hand, feet, bones, head), and human-like representations (for example, humanoid characters) are NOT classified as nature.
- Homogenous Artefacts: Manufactured objects that do not have a CLEARLY identifiable natural texture and that do not depict one of the natural entities described above are completely excluded (for example, a plastic fan, a metal lamp, a plain column with no stone texture visible, a desk painted solid blue with no visible wood grain, a plain shirt made of cotton, a pair of shoes made of leather, a plain scarf made of wool).
  * IMPORTANT: Explicit animal or plant depictions, such as figurines, statues and stuffed toys, are NOT excluded here; they count as Representations of Nature.
- Non-natural Materials: Non-natural or highly processed materials that no longer resemble their natural origin are also excluded (for example, brick, glass, plastic, cement, asphalt, metal, ink, concrete, wool, cotton, leather). 
  * IMPORTANT: Explicit animal or plant depictions, such as figurines, statues and stuffed toys, are NOT excluded here; they count as Representations of Nature.
- Food and Drink Packaging: Containers, wrappers, cans, or boxes, unless the text name or an image of the natural element inside is explicitly printed on the package exterior.
