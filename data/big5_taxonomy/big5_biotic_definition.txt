TAXONOMY REFERENCE: LIFE CATEGORY AXIS

CATEGORY 1: BIOTIC
Definition: All more-than-human living entities, the ecosystems and environments in which they exist and interact, and their representations and derivatives.
Inclusion Scope:
- Flora and its derivatives: Wild or potted plants such as flowers, trees, and vegetables, as well as foods, drinks and materials derived from them (for example, salad, coffee, liqueur, and wood with visible grain).
- Fauna and its derivatives: Wild, domesticated, or bred more-than-human animals such as mammals, molluscs, and insects, as well as foods and materials derived from them (for example, vegetables, butter, honey, meat, eggs, wool, and leather).
- Fungi and Microorganisms: Wild or cultivated mushrooms, moulds, yeasts, bacteria and virus.
- Ecosystems and Environments: Natural or semi-natural environments where the more-than-human living entities exist and interact (for example, forests, coral reefs, nature reserves, urban parks, vegetable gardens, and beaches).
- Representations: Real, stylized, abstract, artistic, scientific or fictional depictions of the more-than-human living entities (for example, landscape paintings, cartoon or stuffed animals and plants, video-game forests, and figurines or statues of fauna and flora).

CATEGORY 2: ABIOTIC
Definition: All non-living physical, chemical, geological, topographical, meteorological, or astrophysical elements that characterize or occur within an ecosystem or natural environment.
Inclusion Scope:
- Hydrological: Water bodies and fluid flows (for example, rivers, ponds, lakes, oceans, and streams).
- Atmospheric and Meteorological: For example, rain, clouds, snow, wind, and sky.
- Geological and Topographical: Large-scale landforms, earth structures, and soil materials (for example, mountains, canyons, cliffs, valleys, caves, dunes, rocks, land, sand, salt).
- Astrophysical: Celestial bodies (for example, sun, stars, moon, and planets).
- Representations: Real, stylized, abstract, artistic, scientific or fictional depictions of the non-living natural elements (for example, a painting or a video-game scene of a mountain range with a blue or cloudy sky).
