TAXONOMY REFERENCE: TANGIBILITY AXIS

CATEGORY 1: MATERIAL
Definition: More-than-human entities, biological or physical processes, substances, and ecosystems that possess physical presence, occupy space, have mass, and can be directly perceived through physical senses in the real world.
Inclusion Scope:
- Real-World Organisms & Ecosystems: Physical plants, animals, fungi, water bodies, geological formations, or green spaces present in the physical environment.
- Biophysical & Environmental Processes: Observable physical phenomena occurring in reality (e.g., active rainfall, wind moving foliage, physical evaporation, real-time sunsets or eclipses).
- Physical Substances & Derivatives: Edible solid/liquid items and natural artefacts possessing physical mass (e.g., prepared meals, sugar, coffee, physical wooden or stone surfaces with visible natural texture).
- Unfiltered Documentary Media: Standard, literal, realistic digital photographs or videos capturing real-world physical landscapes, organisms, or phenomena. A standard camera recording of a real-world entity represents MATERIAL nature.

CATEGORY 2: IMMATERIAL
Definition: Concepts, ideas, abstract entities, and fictional, cultural, scientific, or artistic representations of natural elements, processes, or ecosystems. This encompasses depictions across any medium, whether digital, two-dimensional, or physical three-dimensional objects whose primary purpose is representational or symbolic.
Inclusion Scope:
- Fictional Representations: Depictions that reimagine, anthropomorphize, or fabricate nature in non-literal forms (e.g., video-game environments, animated cartoons, humanized animals, animal-themed toys and plushies, everyday functional or decorative objects shaped to depict natural entities, synthetic or fake plants, costumes).
- Artistic & Cultural Representations: Creative, expressive, or symbolic interpretations of nature across any medium (e.g., drawings, paintings, prints on fabric, stylized or artistic photography, three-dimensional figurines, sculptures or models depicting flora and fauna, songs, poems, proverbs, metaphors).
- Scientific & Digital Mappings: Abstract informational or virtual models of natural features (e.g., infographics, data visualizations, physical geography maps, AI-generated imagery, virtual reality simulations).
